Vertical Video Safe Zone Cheat Sheet (1080 × 1920)

Avoid getting critical subtitles, brand logos, or hook text covered by native app buttons. Here are the exact pixel dimensions.

Platform Top Danger Margin Bottom Danger Margin Right Rail Margin Golden Safe Box
TikTok Top 220 px Bottom 400 px Right 160 px 880 × 1300 px
Instagram Reels Top 200 px Bottom 360 px Right 140 px 900 × 1360 px
YouTube Shorts Top 200 px Bottom 280 px Right 150 px 890 × 1440 px

1. The 3-Second Hook Rule

Place big opening text hooks at 30% to 45% screen height. This is natural viewer eye-level and completely avoids top headers and right action buttons.

2. Caption Placement Sweet Spot

Keep auto-generated captions between 55% and 68% height. Moving them lower guarantees they will be covered by creator handles and sound titles.

3. The 4:5 Feed Crop Danger

Instagram Reels shown on the main feed are cropped to 1080×1350 (clipping 285px top and bottom). Always test with the 4:5 toggle active before publishing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a Safe Zone Previewer necessary?

TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts render thick UI overlays directly on top of videos: like hearts, share arrows, creator usernames, captions, and sound titles. If you place your text or face behind these buttons, your engagement drops. A safe zone previewer lets you verify placement before you post.

Is my video uploaded to any server or cloud storage?

No. This tool runs 100% locally in your web browser using HTML5 File APIs. Your video never leaves your computer, laptop, or phone. It works completely offline and respects client privacy.

What resolution should vertical videos be exported at?

The standard resolution is 1080 × 1920 pixels (9:16 aspect ratio) in MP4 (H.264 / AAC) at 30 or 60 frames per second with a recommended video bitrate between 10 Mbps and 20 Mbps.
